DevOps bridges the traditional gap between software development and IT operations, transforming the SDLC from a rigid, sequential process into a continuous, agile loop. By automating workflows through Continuous Integration and Continuous 

Deployment (CI/CD), DevOps eliminates human error, reduces manual bottlenecks, and accelerates time-to-market. It shifts 
quality and security checks earlier in the cycle ("shifting left"), ensuring that bugs are caught and resolved immediately 

rather than at launch. Ultimately, DevOps changes the SDLC from a slow, siloed handoff into a rapid, collaborative environment where teams can deliver reliable software with high frequency, stability, and speed.
