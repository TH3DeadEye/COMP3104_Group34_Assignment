George Brown College is a public college in downtown Toronto. It was founded in 1967/1968 and named after George Brown, a Canadian politician and newspaper founder. Since 2025, it has been branded as George Brown Polytechnic.

The college has three campuses in downtown Toronto: Casa Loma, St. James and Waterfront. It has about 27,000 full-time students, around 28 percent of them international.
