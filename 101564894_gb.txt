GEORGE BROWN COLLEGE (GEORGE BROWN POLYTECHNIC) 

Overview:
George Brown College (officially George Brown Polytechnic as of 2025) is a premier publicly funded post-secondary institution established in 1967 and located in downtown Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It is highly regarded for its hands-on career preparation and career-focused polytechnic programs.

Key Facts:
- Experiential Learning: Over 88% to 95% of programs include built-in co-ops, field placements, or internships.
- Student Base: Serves approximately 30,000 full-time students, including a vibrant international student population comprising about 42% of the student body.
- Urban Integration: Known for its "City as a Classroom" philosophy, embedding campus locations inside Toronto's economic hubs.

Main Campus Hubs:
1. St. James Campus (King St East): Focuses on Business, Creative Industries, Hospitality & Culinary Arts, and Community Services.
2. Casa Loma Campus (Midtown): Dedicated to Construction, Engineering, Computer Technologies, Skilled Trades, and Fashion.
3. Waterfront Campus aka limberlost (Lake Ontario): State-of-the-art facility housing Health Sciences and the School of Design.
