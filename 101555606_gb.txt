George Brown colleg is a public college in the heart of Toronto, Ontario. It opened in 1967 and is named after George Brown, a newspaper publisher and politician. The school now goes by George Brown Polytechnic


Most programs run out of three main campuses, St.James in downtown core, Case Loma and Waterfront near the lake. Since it's all in the city, most students get to class by TTC.

The college offers diplomas, certification and degrees in areas like technology, business, health scinece and arts. A lof the pgroams focus on hands-on learning instead of old fashon lectures.
