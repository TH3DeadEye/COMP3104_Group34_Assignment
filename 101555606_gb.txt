George Brown College is a public college in Toronto, Ontario. It opened in 1967 and is named after George Brown, a newspaper publisher and politician. The school now goes by George Brown Polytechnic.

Most programs run out of three main campuses: St. James in the downtown core, Casa Loma, and Waterfront near the lake. Since it's all in the city, most students get to class by TTC.

The college offers diplomas, certificates and degrees in areas like technology, business, health sciences and arts. A lot of the programs focus on hands-on learning instead of old fashioned lectures.

I'm in the computer science program and I'm set to graduate in July 2027. The labs and group projects have been the most useful part so far, because they feel a lot like working on a real team.
